
# QR Codes

## 📌 Overview

This folder contains documentation and resources related to the QR-code identification system used in the **Intelligent Hospital Asset Tracking and Management System**.

Each registered hospital asset is assigned a unique QR value that is used to identify the asset during tracking operations.

---

## 🏷️ Asset QR Codes

A unique QR code can be assigned to each hospital asset.

Examples:

- Wheelchair
- Stretcher
- Oxygen cylinder
- Infusion pump
- Portable monitor
- Emergency equipment

The QR label is attached to the corresponding physical asset.

---

## 📍 Location QR Codes

QR labels can also be placed at authorised hospital locations.

Examples:

- Ward
- ICU
- Store
- Maintenance area
- Parking area
- Other authorised locations

Location scanning helps record the latest confirmed location during an asset transaction.

---

## 🔄 QR Tracking Process

```text
Asset Registration
       ↓
Unique Asset ID
       ↓
QR Code Generation
       ↓
QR Label Attached to Asset
       ↓
QR Code Scanned
       ↓
Asset Identified
       ↓
Transaction Recorded
